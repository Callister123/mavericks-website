What is Futsal?

Futsal is a fast-paced indoor sport played on a small court with five players per team.

The sport is recognized by FIFA and is played worldwide, with a strong emphasis on player development and technical ability.

Elements of skill, agility, and teamwork are crucial when playing the game especially when using a smaller, heavier ball than a standard football.
