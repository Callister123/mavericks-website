Welcome to the Mavericks

The Mavericks are a community sports club bringing together players of all ages, abilities and backgrounds. We currently offer two fast-paced indoor sports, futsal and handball, in a safe, positive and inclusive environment where everyone can learn, play and enjoy the game.

Our Sports

What is Futsal?

A fast-paced indoor sport played on a small court with five players per team. Played with a smaller, heavier ball than a standard football, it rewards skill, agility and teamwork.

What is Handball?

A fast-paced indoor team sport played between two teams of seven players (six outfield and a goalkeeper). Players pass, dribble and throw the ball into the opposition's net.
