Who we are

The Mavericks are a community sports club in Milton Keynes. We support players of every age and ability through good coaching, a safe environment and plenty of game time.

Futsal is a fast 5v5 indoor game and the only small-sided format recognised by UEFA and FIFA. Handball is just as quick, and together they give our players new ways to learn and compete.
