About the team

Bucks Mavericks Handball

The Mavericks Women's Handball Team brings together players who want to learn, improve and compete. Whether you are returning to the sport or trying handball for the first time, there is a place for you in the squad.

Bucks Mavericks Handball grew out of the merger of Bucks Bullets Handball and MK Mavericks Futsal. The club runs junior, youth and adult handball in Buckinghamshire.
