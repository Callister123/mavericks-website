What is Handball?

Handball is a fast-paced indoor team sport played between two teams of seven players (six outfield players and one goalkeeper). The objective is simple: score more goals than the opposition by throwing the ball into their net. Combining elements of football, basketball, and netball, handball is known for its speed, skill, and physicality. Players pass, dribble, and shoot while working together in both attack and defence, making it an exciting sport to play and watch.
